Why we built Signal

Most competitor-monitoring tools tell you a page changed. They don't tell you whether it matters. Signal was built for SEOs and content teams who need to know the difference — a title rewrite is not the same as a footer typo, and treating them the same wastes your attention.

Every change we detect gets an SEO Impact Score, a plain-English explanation of why it might matter, and — where it's relevant — a flag for whether it's a link-building opportunity or a sign a competitor is about to outrank you.

We built this free-first: check any page right now, no signup, no card. If it's useful, add pages to a dashboard and get alerted when something changes.

Signal is an independent project, not affiliated with the SEO tools it complements. If something's broken or missing, let us know.
