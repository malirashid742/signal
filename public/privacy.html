Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Signal ("we", "us") provides a tool to check and monitor webpage changes. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • URLs you check or monitor — needed to fetch and diff the page.
  • Email address — only if you sign up for change alerts.
  • Slack webhook URL — only if you add one for alerts, stored to send notifications on your behalf.
  • Browser identifier (cookie) — used to associate your monitored pages with your browser session. No password or personal profile is tied to this.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your monitored URLs or email with third parties.
  • We don't track you across other websites.
  • We don't run ads or ad-tracking scripts.

Screenshots

When you monitor a page, Signal captures a screenshot of the publicly visible page for visual comparison purposes. Screenshots are stored to show you before/after changes and are not shared publicly unless you explicitly use the public change-history link feature for that page.

Data retention

Monitoring history and screenshots are kept for as long as your monitor is active. You can stop monitoring a page at any time, which removes it from active tracking.

Third-party services we use

  • Email delivery (Gmail SMTP) — to send you change alerts, if you opt in.
  • Slack — only if you provide a webhook URL for alerts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
